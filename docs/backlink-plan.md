# ParvoMaps Backlink Plan
Opened 2026-09-01. The lever for indexing, not more pages.

## The problem this solves
Google Search Console, 2026-09-01:
- 499 URLs in the sitemap (51 state pages + 387 state/county/disease pages + blog)
- **359 "Discovered, currently not indexed"** — Google found them and chose not to crawl
- Only 5 outbreak pages have ever gotten impressions

Linking is NOT the issue. Verified: homepage → /outbreaks → 51 states → county/disease
pages. Nothing is orphaned. The issue is **domain authority**. Crawl budget follows
authority, and the site went from a handful of pages to 499 in about two weeks on a
young domain with almost no inbound links.

**More pages will not fix this. Links will.** A handful of real backlinks would unlock
hundreds of already-written pages.

## Proof the pages are good, once served
| Page | Impressions | Clicks | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage | 6,731 | 1,861 | **27.6%** |
| /outbreaks/georgia | 54 | 10 | **18.5%** |
| /outbreaks/missouri/rabies | 58 | 6 | 10.3% |
| /outbreaks/oregon | 39 | 4 | 10.3% |
| /outbreaks/florida | 52 | 4 | 7.7% |

Site-wide average CTR in Search Console is 3-5%. These are 3-6x that. The template
converts. It is a distribution problem, not a quality problem.

The 27.6% homepage CTR is branded/navigational search: people are typing the name and
clicking because ParvoMaps is the only free public parvo outbreak map. That is the moat,
and it is also the pitch.

## Target list (ranked by effort-to-value)

### 1. Local news covering an outbreak we mapped — HIGHEST VALUE
One local TV or paper story is worth more than a hundred new pages. We have the data
before they do. When a cluster shows up in a metro area, email the health/pets reporter
with the map link and the case count. Free, exclusive, visual, and they need a source.

### 2. Veterinary clinics
Cold email is realistic here because the ask is small: "we track parvo cases in your
county, free, here is your county page, link it on your parvo info page if it's useful."
Start with clinics in the states that already index (GA, MO, FL, OR, WA).

### 3. State veterinary medical associations
Higher authority than individual clinics. Many have a "resources" or "public health
links" page. Same pitch, one email per state.

### 4. Animal shelters and breed rescues
High link volume, lower authority each, but they care about parvo more than anyone and
they publish constantly.

### 5. Reddit and forums — r/dogs, r/vet, breed subs
Nofollow, so no direct link equity, but real referral traffic and it seeds branded
search, which is what the homepage already runs on. NOTE: do not pitch. Answer an actual
outbreak question and link the county page as the source. See [[reddit-outreach-venues]]
for venues and the no-pitch rule.

### 6. University vet schools / extension programs
Hardest to land, highest authority. Worth one email each to the ones in states with real
pin density.

## Rules
- One email at a time, personalized. No blast. A mailmerge to 200 clinics gets us
  marked as spam and burns the domain.
- The ask is always "here is your county's page," never "please link to us."
- Never pay for links. A young domain cannot survive a manual action.
- Log every outreach below so we do not double-contact.

## While links build
- **Stop generating pages.** 359 unindexed already; more only splits the same crawl budget.
- Manually request indexing on the ~10 state pages with the most pins (URL Inspection →
  Request Indexing, roughly 10/day quota).
- County pages with a single pin will likely never index. That is fine, they are not the
  point. The state pages are.
